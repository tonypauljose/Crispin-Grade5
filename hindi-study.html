Say it out loud and the app tells you if it came out right — word by word.

How this works

Hindi is not his first language, so guessing "I read it correctly" is how a wrong pronunciation sticks. Here the browser listens in Hindi and compares what it heard with the word on the card.

१ · सुनोTap 🔊 to hear it, 🐢 for slow. Copy the sound.
२ · बोलोTap 🎤 and say it. Speak straight away, close to the mic.
३ · जाँचोIt shows what it heard. In a sentence, wrong words turn red.
४ · फिर सेAnything you miss lands in मुश्किल शब्द to drill.
Tap the button and say नमस्ते to check the microphone.
